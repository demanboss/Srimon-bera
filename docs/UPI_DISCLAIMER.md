# MOON Elite — Legal UPI & Regulatory Compliance Disclaimer

## 1. Regulatory Status & TPAP Disclosure
- **MOON Elite** operates either in **SANDBOX MODE** (for development, audit, and demonstration purposes) or in **PRODUCTION MODE** via officially authorized agreements with an RBI-licensed Payment Aggregator (PA) or Sponsor PSP Bank operating as a Third-Party Application Provider (TPAP) under the NPCI Unified Payments Interface framework.
- MOON Elite is **not** a bank, nor does it hold user deposits or operate a private banking ledger.
- In **SANDBOX MODE**, all transaction identifiers, bank references, and UPI responses are simulated within a segregated testing environment. No real funds are debited from or credited to any real bank account.

## 2. UPI PIN Confidentiality (NPCI Mandate)
- NPCI regulations strictly prohibit any entity other than the official NPCI Common Library (MPIN SDK) from prompting for or capturing a user's UPI PIN.
- MOON Elite will **never** ask you to disclose your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, OTP, or CVV.

## 3. Rewards & Wallet Clarification
- "Rewards Balance" or "Cashback Points" represent promotional non-interest-bearing platform credits redeemable according to published promotional campaign rules.
- Rewards balances do not constitute bank savings deposits, escrowed wallets, or prepaid payment instruments (PPI) unless explicitly managed under an authorized PPI license.
