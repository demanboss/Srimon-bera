# MOON Elite — Comprehensive Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction & Compliance Scope
MOON Elite is committed to the highest standards of data privacy and financial data protection in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions on Digital Payments**, and National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) circulars.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Identity & Contact Details:** Full Name, verified mobile phone number, email address.
- **Device & Telemetry Data:** Device model, operating system version, unique device fingerprint, IP address, and emulator/root security detection signals.
- **Payment Transaction Meta:** Virtual Payment Address (VPA / UPI ID), merchant references, transaction amount, timestamp, and NPCI RRN.
- **Camera Access:** Requested exclusively when you initiate the "Scan & Pay" feature to capture optical UPI QR codes. Images are processed live on-device and are NEVER saved or uploaded to our servers.

> **CRITICAL SECURITY PROMISE**: MOON Elite NEVER asks for, records, logs, or stores your secret UPI PIN, bank login passwords, OTPs, or debit/credit card CVV.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
1. To facilitate UPI transaction initiation through authorized TPAP/PSP banking partners.
2. To compute eligible cashback rewards and promotional points based on authorized campaign rules.
3. To monitor transaction patterns for fraud prevention, AML compliance, and prevention of multi-account abuse.
4. To communicate transaction receipts, security warnings, and customer support resolutions.

## 4. Third-Party Disclosures & Payment Partners
We collaborate exclusively with RBI-authorized Payment Aggregators (PA), Third-Party Application Providers (TPAP), and sponsor PSP Banks (e.g., YES Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank).

## 5. Account Deletion & Legal Data Retention
Under Section 30 of the PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act) and RBI guidelines, financial transaction records and audit logs must be securely retained for a minimum statutory period of **5 years**.
When a user submits an **Account Deletion Request**:
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) including name and email is scrubbed/anonymized.
- Active authentication tokens and device linkages are immediately revoked.
- Immutable payment and audit ledger records are quarantined for statutory regulatory compliance.
